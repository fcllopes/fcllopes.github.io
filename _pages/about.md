Research
======

My current research is an amplitude analysis of the \(D^+ \to K^- K^+ \pi^+\) decay using LHCb Run 2 data. I also contribute to the Real-Time Analysis (RTA) project, developing software for the trigger system and monitoring interfaces for the Electromagnetic Calorimeter calibration.

During my undergraduate studies at UNICAMP, I worked on CP violation in charm decays and neutrino oscillations with Prof. Dr. Patricia Camargo Magalhães.
