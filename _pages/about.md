I am a Ph.D. student in Physics at [Universidade Estadual de Campinas (UNICAMP)](https://www.unicamp.br/) and a member of the [LHCb Collaboration](https://lhcb.web.cern.ch/) at [CERN](https://home.cern/), currently based in Saint-Genis-Pouilly, France. I am affiliated through the [Centro Brasileiro de Pesquisas Físicas (CBPF)](https://cbpf.br/).

Research
======

My current research focuses on an amplitude analysis of the \(D^+ \to K^- K^+ \pi^+\) decay using LHCb Run 2 data. I also contribute to the Real-Time Analysis (RTA) project at LHCb, including software development for the trigger system and monitoring interfaces for the Electromagnetic Calorimeter calibration.

Previously, I studied CP violation in charm decays and neutrino oscillations during my undergraduate work at UNICAMP.
